Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations & Disclosure Requirements)

1. Appointment of Mr. Sanjeev Shankaran, presently holding the position of Vice President -

   shall be included in the category of Senior Management Personnel.

2. Mr. Dastgir Shaikh, Vice President - General Merchandising, shall assume the role of Vice President
   - FMCG and continue to be categorized as Senior Managerial Personnel of the Company.

3. Mr. Sachin Jaolekar, Vice President – FMCG, will take up the position of Vice President - Category
   Management at Avenue E-Commerce Limited, a subsidiary of the Company and consequently cease
   to be part of Senior Management Personnel of the Company.
